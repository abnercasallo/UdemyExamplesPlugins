# Brand Guide — Office Standards Plugin

Complete visual identity reference for all four themes. This guide governs how each theme is applied across PowerPoint, Word, and Excel to ensure consistent, professional-looking documents.

---

## Principles

**Consistency over creativity.** The goal of these themes is to make all your documents look like they came from the same hand, even when created at different times or by different people.

**Three-layer approach.** Every theme is built around three color roles:
- **Primary** — The dominant color. Used for headers, title bars, and major structural elements.
- **Accent** — The supporting color. Used for highlights, totals, call-to-action elements, and chart accents.
- **Surface** — The background tint. Used for alternating rows, section backgrounds, and cards.

**Font discipline.** Each theme uses exactly two fonts: one for headings (bold personality) and one for body text (readable neutrality). Never mix in additional fonts.

## Theme 1: Corporativo Azul

**Tagline**: Authority and trust.
**Personality**: This theme says "we know what we're doing." It's formal without being cold, classic without being dated. The navy conveys depth and reliability; the gold accent adds a touch of distinction.

**Primary color**: Navy Blue `#1B3A6B`
**Accent color**: Gold `#C9A84C`
**Background surface**: Ice Blue `#EEF2F9`
**Body text**: Dark Navy `#0D1F3C`
**Secondary text**: Medium Gray `#6B7280`
**Page/slide background**: White `#FFFFFF`

**Heading font**: Garamond Bold → fallback: Georgia Bold
**Body font**: Calibri Regular → fallback: Times New Roman Regular

**Ideal for**: Annual financial reports, board presentations, legal briefs, investor decks, executive summaries, compliance documents.

**Not ideal for**: Creative pitches, startup decks, casual internal updates.

**Application rules:**
- Title slides and section headers: Navy background `#1B3A6B`, white text `#FFFFFF`
- Body slides/pages: White background, Navy headings, Dark Navy body text
- Table headers: Navy fill `#1B3A6B`, white bold text
- Alternating table rows: Ice Blue `#EEF2F9` / White `#FFFFFF`
- Total/summary rows: Gold fill `#C9A84C`, Dark Navy text, bold
- Accent shapes and dividers: Gold `#C9A84C`
- Chart primary series: Navy `#1B3A6B`
- Chart secondary series: Gold `#C9A84C`
- Chart tertiary series: Medium Blue `#4A7BC8`

## Theme 2: Oscuro Profesional

**Tagline**: Precision and impact.
**Personality**: Modern and confident. The dark charcoal background creates drama and focus; the electric blue accent is sharp and energetic. This theme commands attention in a room.

**Primary color**: Charcoal `#2D2D2D`
**Accent color**: Electric Blue `#4A9EFF`
**Background surface**: Off-White `#F5F5F5`
**Body text on light**: Near Black `#1A1A1A`
**Body text on dark**: Soft White `#E8E8E8`
**Card/sidebar surface (dark)**: Dark Gray `#3D3D3D`

**Heading font**: Arial Bold → fallback: Helvetica Neue Bold
**Body font**: Arial Regular → fallback: Helvetica Neue Regular

**Ideal for**: Sales pitch decks, technology presentations, SaaS product demos, investor pitches, startup fundraising.

**Not ideal for**: Formal legal documents, conservative finance reports, government or institutional materials.

**Application rules:**
- Title and impact slides: Charcoal background `#2D2D2D`, Soft White text `#E8E8E8`
- Content slides (light variant): Off-White background `#F5F5F5`, Near Black headings, body text
- Table headers: Charcoal fill `#2D2D2D`, Soft White bold text
- Alternating table rows: Light Gray `#F0F0F0` / White `#FFFFFF`
- Total/summary rows: Electric Blue fill `#4A9EFF`, White text, bold
- Accent shapes: Electric Blue `#4A9EFF`
- Chart primary series: Electric Blue `#4A9EFF`
- Chart secondary series: Charcoal `#2D2D2D`
- Chart tertiary series: Coral Red `#FF6B6B`

**pptx note**: Alternate between dark title slides and light content slides for visual rhythm. Use Electric Blue as the accent on both variants.

## Theme 3: Claro Minimalista

**Tagline**: Clarity and ease.
**Personality**: Breathing room and simplicity. White space is generous, the teal is calm and approachable, and nothing competes for attention. This theme puts the content front and center.

**Primary color**: Teal `#2D9C8C`
**Accent color**: Slate `#4A5568`
**Background surface**: Light Gray `#F0F2F5`
**Body text**: Dark Slate `#2D3748`
**Secondary text**: Medium Gray `#718096`
**Page/slide background**: White `#FFFFFF`

**Heading font**: Trebuchet MS Bold → fallback: Century Gothic Bold
**Body font**: Trebuchet MS Regular → fallback: Calibri Regular

**Ideal for**: Internal process documents, HR materials, operational reports, training manuals, team updates, onboarding materials.

**Not ideal for**: High-stakes external presentations, luxury brand materials, formal financial filings.

**Application rules:**
- Title slides: White background, Teal title text `#2D9C8C`, Slate subtitle
- Section headers: Light Gray background `#F0F2F5`, Teal heading
- Body pages: White background, Teal H1, Slate H2, Dark Slate body
- Table headers: Teal fill `#2D9C8C`, White bold text
- Alternating table rows: Light Gray `#F0F2F5` / White `#FFFFFF`
- Total/summary rows: Slate fill `#4A5568`, White text, bold
- Accent shapes and horizontal rules: Teal `#2D9C8C`
- Chart primary series: Teal `#2D9C8C`
- Chart secondary series: Slate `#4A5568`
- Chart tertiary series: Light Teal `#68D8C8`

## Theme 4: Moderno Vivo

**Tagline**: Energy and boldness.
**Personality**: Vibrant and unapologetically expressive. The deep purple signals creativity and vision; the orange accent brings warmth and urgency. This theme is designed to be remembered.

**Primary color**: Deep Purple `#4B1A7D`
**Accent color**: Vibrant Orange `#FF6B35`
**Background surface**: Lavender `#F3EEFF`
**Body text**: Dark Purple `#2D0F4A`
**Body text on dark**: Off-White `#FFF8F5`
**Page/slide background**: White `#FFFFFF`

**Heading font**: Montserrat Bold → fallback: Trebuchet MS Bold
**Body font**: Lato Regular → fallback: Calibri Regular

**Ideal for**: Marketing campaign decks, brand launches, creative pitches, agency presentations, product announcements.

**Not ideal for**: Conservative corporate environments, legal or compliance documents, highly data-dense reports.

**Application rules:**
- Cover and impact slides: Deep Purple background `#4B1A7D`, Off-White text `#FFF8F5`
- Content slides: White background, Deep Purple headings, Dark Purple body text
- Table headers: Deep Purple fill `#4B1A7D`, Off-White bold text
- Alternating table rows: Lavender `#F3EEFF` / White `#FFFFFF`
- Total/summary rows: Vibrant Orange fill `#FF6B35`, White text, bold
- Accent shapes, borders, and callouts: Vibrant Orange `#FF6B35`
- Chart primary series: Deep Purple `#4B1A7D`
- Chart secondary series: Vibrant Orange `#FF6B35`
- Chart tertiary series: Medium Purple `#9B59B6`

**pptx note**: Use Deep Purple background slides sparingly (cover, section dividers). Too many dark slides reduce readability.

## Cross-document consistency checklist

Before sharing a set of documents from the same project, verify:

- [ ] All files use the same theme number
- [ ] Table header colors match across docx and xlsx
- [ ] Heading fonts are the same (or using the same fallback)
- [ ] Primary color appears as H1/title in docx and as header fill in xlsx
- [ ] Accent color is used for totals in xlsx and for callout shapes in pptx
- [ ] No document uses colors from a different theme
- [ ] Charts across all files use the same color order (primary first, accent second)
